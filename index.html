Hello, I'm Chenshu Liu (Chen)

"AI and biomedical science can speak the same language—I'm here to make that conversation happen."

I am a neuroscientist 🧠 and statistician 📊 in mind, an engineer ⚙️ at hand, and an artist/photographer 📷 at heart. I am a curious innovator exploring AI integration in Health Science & Humanities, merging computer science, medical engineering, heritage science, neuroscience, etc.

I am currently a PhD student at Liu Lab at National University of Singapore (NUS), supervised by Professor Yuxin Liu, working on soft wearable devices for continual biomarker monitoring

I am also a researcher at Terasaki Institute, working on wearable biomedical devices and interfaces to integrate AI components in medicine to foster a smart-healthcare ecosystem, supervised by Professor Yangzhi Zhu, as well as on AI in immunology, supervised by Professor Chongming Jiang.

I also serve as a technical consultant for the Smart Textile group led by Professor Wei Cao at ARCS on evaluating robustness of wearable smart textiles from the lens of consumer aspects.

Previously, I held a research fellow appointment at Laboratory for Smart and Additive Manufacturing at CSUN, working on AI applications in additive manufacturing (AM) and deploying knowledge-graph driven methods for efficient human machine interaction in AM process, supervised by Professor Bingbing Li.

I did my Masters in Bioengineering and a dual bachelor’s in Neuroscience and Statistics at University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA).
